What Is Hapkido?

Hapkido is a Korean martial art built around three core ideas: harmony of force, circular motion, and water principle. Instead of meeting force with force, you redirect it.

In practice, you'll learn joint locks, throws, kicks, strikes, falls, and weapons forms. It's an effective self-defence system that's also a deep technical art with a clear belt progression.

It suits people who like learning a system over time — beginners are welcome at every class.

What to Expect at CMA&F

Class size

8–18 students with 1–2 instructors.

Experience level

Every class accommodates complete beginners.

What to wear

Comfortable activewear. Uniform optional for your first month.

Progression

10-level belt system. Promotion testing every ~6 months.
