Five Fitness Classes

Each class is led by a qualified coach. All levels welcome — modify or push, your choice.

Recovery

Mobility & Flexibility

#FLEXfit

Move better, hurt less. Active flexibility and joint range work.

Strength

Strength & Endurance

#POWERfit

Progressive strength training in a small-group format.

Cardio

Cardio Boxing

#FIGHTINGfit

Cardio boxing with martial arts elements. Energy, sweat, fun.

HIIT

HIIT Intervals

#HIITfit

High-intensity intervals — efficient and effective.

Outdoor

Outdoor Bootcamp

#JERRAbootcamp

6am outdoor bootcamp at Jerrabomberra. Best way to start the day.

Personal Training

One-on-One Coaching

Tailored programming for specific goals — strength, weight loss, return from injury, sport prep. Sessions with qualified CMA&F coaches at either club.
