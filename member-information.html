Membership & Payment Terms

Direct debit runs fortnightly. Memberships continue automatically until cancelled (see below). Prices include public holidays and the Christmas shutdown — there are no extra weekly fees.

For the full legal terms see Terms & Conditions.

Code of Conduct

Our Values

Respect, discipline, community, growth, inclusion, authenticity, fun, lifetime learning.

Member Conduct

Treat instructors, training partners, the venue and yourself with respect. Show up on time, in clean uniform where applicable. Pay attention during instruction. Train safely — control your techniques to your partner's level.

Bullying, harassment or discrimination are grounds for immediate review of membership.

Suspension & Injury Policy

If you're injured or unable to train for an extended period, you can request a suspension of your membership. Submit the request in writing (email is fine). Suspensions are processed for the upcoming billing cycle.

Minimum suspension period: 2 weeks. Maximum: 12 weeks per calendar year without medical evidence.

Cancellation Policy

Memberships can be cancelled at any time with 14 days written notice. Use the cancellation form below or email members@canberramartialarts.com.au.
