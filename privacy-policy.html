Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 May 2026

Canberra Martial Arts & Fitness Pty Ltd (CMA&F, we, us, our) is committed to protecting the privacy of our members, prospective members, and website visitors. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and store personal information.

1. Information we collect

We collect personal information you provide directly — name, contact details, date of birth, emergency contact, payment details, medical information relevant to training, and class attendance.

2. How we use your information

To deliver classes and manage memberships, to communicate about classes and events, to process payments, to keep our members safe, and to comply with our legal obligations.

3. Disclosure

We disclose personal information only to service providers who help us operate the club (payment processors, membership platform, email tools) and only to the extent necessary. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Storage and security

Information is stored in secure systems with access controls. We retain records only as long as needed for legal, contractual and operational purposes.

5. Your rights

You can request access to or correction of your personal information at any time by contacting us.

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to remember preferences and to measure how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

7. Children

For members under 18, personal information is collected from a parent or guardian, who consents to its use under this policy.

8. Contact

Questions about your data? Contact our Privacy Officer at members@canberramartialarts.com.au.
