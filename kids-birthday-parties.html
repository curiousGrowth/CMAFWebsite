Parent FAQs

Can we bring our own food?

Yes — bring cake and party food. Package 3 includes catering if you'd rather not.

How many kids can attend?

Minimum 10, comfortable maximum 20. Talk to us for larger groups.

Do you provide invitations?

Package 3 includes custom printable invitations.

Can children with special needs join?

Yes — let us know what we can do to make the party work for your child.

Is the venue accessible?

Our Woden location requires stair access. Please contact us to discuss requirements for guests with mobility needs, including options at our Coombs location.
