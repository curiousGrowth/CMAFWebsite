About CMA&F

More Than a Gym. More Like a Family.

CMA&F has been training Canberrans since 2011. Here's our story.

Vision

To empower self-confidence through self-awareness and a passion for martial arts and fitness.

Mission

To provide fun, safe and engaging martial arts and fitness programs unlike anything else available.

Our Values

  1. Respect — for self, for training partners, for the art.
  2. Discipline — show up, work hard, do it again.
  3. Community — every member is a contributor, not a customer.
  4. Growth — we measure progress over performance.
  5. Inclusion — three-year-olds to sixty-year-olds, all welcome.
  6. Authenticity — qualified instructors teaching real martial arts.
  7. Fun — if you're not enjoying it, we're doing it wrong.
  8. Lifetime learning — a black belt is a beginning, not an end.

How CMA&F Began

Tom started CMA&F in 2011 with a small Hapkido class at a community hall in Phillip. Fifteen years later, the club teaches five martial arts styles and a full fitness program across two locations.

The reason hasn't changed: build a place where people who would never walk into a typical gym feel like they belong. Kids who are shy. Adults who haven't trained since school. Parents wanting something for their three-year-old. The club is built around them.
