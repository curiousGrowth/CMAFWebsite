How to Pick the Right Martial Art For You

By Tom Adam · 12 May 2026 · 6 min read · Martial Arts

The most common question we get from new visitors is: "Which style should I do?" Here's the honest answer — it depends on what you want, not what's "best".

Start with the why

If you want self-defence, look at Hapkido or Krav Maga. If you want competition and structured progression, Taekwondo. If you want grappling and live problem-solving, BJJ. If you want tradition and weapons, Kummooyeh.

Try before you decide

Reading about a style only gets you so far. The 21-Day Trial exists for this reason — you can sample every class and see which one clicks.

It's OK to switch

Half our long-term members started in a different style than the one they ended up loving. The point is to start.
